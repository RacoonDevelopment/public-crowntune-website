Content and Metadata Guidelines

These guidelines apply to music, artwork, credits, descriptions, and other material submitted to CROWNTUNE DISTRIBUTION LLC. They form part of the Terms of Service. We may reject or remove a release that does not meet them or a Platform's current rules.

Rights and originality

Submit only recordings you own or are authorized to distribute, with permission for every sample, beat, artwork element, featured performance, and other protected contribution. Keep written proof, including contributor permissions and any licenses for samples, beats, or artwork. A nonexclusive beat license must authorize the intended distribution and does not necessarily authorize automated rights claims against other people using the same beat. A royalty-free label, public online availability, or purchase of a file does not necessarily give you distribution rights. Do not upload unauthorized remixes, bootlegs, karaoke copies, soundalikes, stolen recordings, or content that impersonates another artist. Crowntune does not distribute cover songs, even if you obtained a mechanical license. A claim that a composition is in the public domain does not establish that someone else's recording or arrangement is free to use; supply evidence of the rights in every part of a proposed release.

AI generated music

Crowntune does not accept music generated in whole or in part by artificial intelligence. That includes recordings with AI-generated vocals or instrumentals, synthetic performances, or cloned voices. Ordinary production tools such as equalizers, noise reduction, and mastering effects do not by themselves make a recording AI generated, but a tool's label does not decide eligibility; we may ask how the recording was made. Do not use a person's voice, likeness, or identity without permission.

Accurate metadata and artwork

Use the correct artist names, titles, contributors, explicit-content flags, language, release dates, and recording identifiers. Do not add another artist's name to attract searches or use misleading “official,” “live,” “remix,” or genre claims. Do not submit duplicate releases, repeated near-identical tracks intended to game discovery, or unrelated keywords in titles or credits. Artwork must be yours or licensed and must match the release. Avoid trademarks, logos, personal information, or third-party images without permission.

Platform and legal standards

Do not submit unlawful content, threats, targeted harassment, material that sexually exploits minors, material that violates another person's privacy or publicity rights, or content intended to deceive listeners or Platforms. Mark explicit lyrics and artwork accurately. A lawful artistic reference or explicit-content label does not excuse infringement or another Platform violation. Platform specifications for audio, artwork, spelling, and metadata can differ and change. We may request revisions or documentation, limit a release's destinations, or decline it. Approval is a distribution decision, not a legal opinion about your rights.

Audio quality and release changes

Submit complete, playable files that meet the specifications given during submission. Do not disguise a low-quality file by converting it to a larger format. Avoid accidental silence, clipping, corrupted audio, unrelated files, and misleading previews. If a substantial change creates a different recording, a new identifier or release submission may be required. Transfers should preserve valid identifiers and accurate original release dates where Platform rules permit.
